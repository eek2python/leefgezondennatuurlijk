I would like to extend my Django affiliate website with a generic capacity system for storage containers and future product categories.

The goal is to support products consisting of one or multiple containers while automatically generating user-friendly capacity text and total capacity.

--------------------------------------------
1. Product model
--------------------------------------------

Products may now contain an optional field:

"capacities": [700,700,1500]

The values represent milliliters.

Examples:

Single container

"capacities": [800]

Three equal containers

"capacities": [1000,1000,1000]

Mixed set

"capacities": [370,370,640,1040]

Products without this field must continue working normally.

--------------------------------------------
